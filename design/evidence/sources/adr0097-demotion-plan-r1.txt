## Scope

A0 only: allocator-owned preparation of a complete release set and its publication resources.
Use the same coalescing/edit rule for batch and scalar releases and prefix restoration; do not
copy allocator logic into `compact.rs` or leave a second "safe" implementation beside the old one.
Directory/page preconditions and split scratch remain at the existing paged-map owner. Record,
bin, free-interval and max-heap invariants remain at `FreeSpanStore`.

The proposed private shape is a fallible prepare operation returning one non-Clone, nonpersisted
allocator-owned prepared-release value, and a consuming publish operation with no recoverable
result. The value owns validated coalesced interval/edit data and reserved bookkeeping. It is not
fabricable by callers and is invalid after intervening allocator mutation; the prepare/publish
sequence permits no re-entry, callback or await. Native Drop/unwind is not cleanup or rollback.

**Interface review gate before coding:** pin exact Rust signatures/visibility, reservation cause
representation, all affected scalar/symmetric paths and the infallible remainder. Do not widen a
public API/error contract or escape the two-file source surface without stopping for review. A
whole release batch must account for mutual adjacency/coalescing and index edits, not merely run
independent preflights against a stale initial free list. Expected failures stay typed pre-write
errors; converting them to post-write panics does not satisfy this plan.

A0 does not acquire graph edge/value targets. A1 will reserve cancellation bookkeeping before
acquisitions, then prepare retirement against the resulting allocator state; it cannot reuse a
pre-acquisition release plan. Allocation growth/unreferenced bytes may remain after failed
preparation, but canonical free ownership/counts must not be partially published.

Non-goals: physical caller wiring; native LTB/root or value retirement; logical movement/cursors;
deletion/assertion removal; matching/header/tag005/GAP007 changes; Graph/queue/PocketIC integration;
new allocator/module/dependency/cache/persisted state; new benchmark, survey/persistence, 16K
replay, full-suite reassurance, threshold/layout/compatibility changes, commits or merging.
